Privacy Policy

This page explains what information YouPlay handles, how it is used, when it may be shared, and the choices you have when using the app.

Last updated: July 19, 2026

YouPlay ("YouPlay," "we," "our," or "us") provides a macOS app for AI video translation. This Privacy Policy explains what information YouPlay handles, how it is used, when it may be shared, and what choices you have when using the app.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the YouPlay app and related pages or support channels that link to it.

By using YouPlay, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We prioritize secure on-device processing and only utilize advanced cloud models for maximum fidelity when requested.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, YouPlay may handle:

  • app preferences, language settings, and voice selections
  • YouTube video URLs and captions metadata processed during translation
  • subscription or purchase status needed to unlock premium features
  • text or request data sent when using online translation and audio providers
  • locally cached audio segments to improve playback performance

When using offline voices, all processing is done locally on your Mac. No video or audio data is sent to our servers.

3. How We Use Information

YouPlay uses information to:

  • provide synchronized video dubbing and translation features
  • save your preferences and playback settings
  • support translation and audio generation
  • process purchases, subscriptions, and restore premium access
  • maintain app reliability, security, and basic operations

4. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information.

YouPlay may rely on or share limited information with third-party services in these cases:

  • Apple App Store and RevenueCat help manage subscriptions, purchases, and restoring premium access
  • translation and audio providers may process text if you enable online features
  • third-party video platforms (like YouTube) provide the source video and captions

Third-party services follow their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Your Choices

Most of YouPlay's operational data, including cached audio and preferences, is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain a central database of your watching history.

You may be able to reduce or remove data by:

  • clearing the audio cache in the app settings
  • resetting app preferences
  • choosing to use strictly offline voices instead of online providers
  • managing subscriptions through the Apple App Store
  • uninstalling the app

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information used by YouPlay. By offering robust local processing alongside opt-in cloud models, you have full control over what data is transmitted over the network. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may update the date above and, where appropriate, provide notice in the app or through the relevant app store listing.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us via our Support channels.
